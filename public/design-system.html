iS iSMART Club

The iSmart Club design system

A dark-only, neon-green sports theme for the club-management platform — staff, players, organigram, meetings, tasks, regulations, budget and planning. Depth comes from surface layering, hairline borders and the occasional green glow — never gradients or heavy decoration. One electric green, used with restraint, on a layered near-black canvas, set in Rubik.

Tier 1 — non-negotiable

Six rules that make it cohere

These apply to every screen, component and asset. Break one and the UI stops looking like iSmart Club. Everything else in this document is an application of them.

Rule 01

Two surface colors only

#131313 is the page, #181818 is any element on it. Never a third structural fill — separate nested regions with a border, or the single nested fill #1d1d1d.

Rule 02

Green is for buttons only

The neon green appears on primary buttons, the logo mark, and as success status. Not on input focus, colored icons, accent text, tags, tabs, links or progress bars.

Rule 03

One calm accent — blue

Blue is the single non-button accent: tags, selected controls, links, active indicators, stat deltas, inline “+ add”. No rainbow of decorative hues.

Rule 04

Inputs are empty + bordered

Text inputs, selects and secondary buttons are transparent with a #404040 edge. Solid fills are reserved for the primary green (and destructive red) button.

Rule 05

Focus is neutral

On focus, only the border color changes — to a lighter gray #5a5a5a. No ring, no shadow, no fill, and never a green or blue focus.

Rule 06

Go light on weight

Default weight is regular (400). Emphasis comes from size, color and spacing — not bold. Semibold (600) is for big titles and stat numbers; avoid 700.

Cards

A card is a 1px border + rounded-lg, in one of two resting fills depending on whether it is static or navigable. No left-accent borders, no colored fills, no gradients.

Static card

A panel that just displays content — stat tiles, form sections, tables. No fill, transparent over the page, defined by its border alone. Hover only brightens the border.

Foundations

Iconography

Lucide, via lucide-react. Stroke icons at 2px, 16px default (14 in dense rows, 18–20 for emphasis). Icons inherit currentColor — muted when idle, white when active, dark on green buttons. Never tint a decorative icon green.

Calendar · Users · Clock · Settings · Briefcase · Plus · Filter · Search — a slice of the common glyph set. The logo iS monogram is the one place green + bold is intentional. Emoji are never used.

Content

Content & voice

The product UI is French-first. The tone is operational and direct — an internal back-office / coaching tool, not marketing.

LanguageFrench, set once and kept consistent — Planification, Joueurs, Réunions, Fiche de poste, Budget.
ToneNouns or short imperatives — Ajouter, Enregistrer, Fermer. No exclamation, no first person, no marketing voice.
CasingSentence case for descriptions & helper text; short button labels in title-ish case; UPPERCASE only for small overlines & metric labels.
NumbersIDs / phones in mono; hero stats in Rubik semibold, white; counts as bare integers (“8 membres”); money-in green, money-out red.
FeedbackA control says exactly what happens — Enregistrer, then a toast that confirms “Événement ajouté au planning”.
Tokens are the single source of truth — restyle the whole system by editing src/styles/index.css, not components. This reference mirrors that file and the ismartcup-design-system skill. Dark-only by design; there is no light theme.
